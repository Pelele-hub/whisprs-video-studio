## Objective
To generate hypnotic, high-retention advisory voiceovers for short reels (20 to 30 seconds) using VoiceStudio. The narration must never abruptly drop in pitch or stop instantly; every phrase must remain connected to the next.

## Strict Formatting & Cadence Rules

1. **WORD COUNT**: Exactly 40 to 50 words total (calibrated for a 20–30 second reel at a slow 110 WPM delivery).
2. **NO HARD PUNCTUATION**: Never use periods (`.`), exclamation marks (`!`), or question marks (`?`). Hard punctuation triggers automatic pitch drops and sudden full stops in neural TTS models.
3. **LOWERCASE ONLY**: Write the entire script in lowercase to prevent capital-letter opening and closing pitch inflections.
4. **SUSPENDED PITCH PAUSES**: Use ONLY three dots (`...`) or single dashes (`-`) to create micro-pauses (200ms–300ms) that keep the voice pitch suspended in the air.
5. **MID-CLAUSE BREAKS**: Split sentences mid-thought or mid-clause. Never break where a natural grammatical end occurs. Force phrase 1 to hang until phrase 2 picks it up.
6. **NO HARD LINE BREAKS**: Keep the text flowing continuously as a single stream of thought.

## Tone & Style
- Calm, direct, deeply reflective, and advising.
- Feels like a quiet truth being shared rather than a speech.

## Output Format
Provide ONLY the raw pre-processed script ready to copy-paste directly into VoiceStudio without any introductory text, titles, quotes, or meta-commentary.
